How to Play line.line

Objective: Players take turns connecting pairs of points with straight lines. The player who draws the last valid line of a round scores that round's points and starts the next round!

Round 1 (1 pt): Every new line must cross exactly 1 existing line.
Round 2 (2 pts): Every new line must cross exactly 2 existing lines.
Round 3 (3 pts): Every new line must cross exactly 3 existing lines.
  • Round Winner Keeps Initiative: If you draw the final valid line in Round 1 or Round 2, you earn the round point(s) and immediately make the opening move of the next round.
  • Turn-by-Turn Replay & Move Analysis: After any game, click Watch Replay & Move Analysis to step through every move, see its rating (Best, Good, Inaccuracy, Blunder), and view the engine's recommended line.
  • Download & Upload Dots File: Export a `.json` file of the 12 points anytime and upload it in the lobby to replay that exact board setup.
